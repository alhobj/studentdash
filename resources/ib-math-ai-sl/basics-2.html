Find a slope

Slope = change in y ÷ change in x.

Step 1

Give me a hint

Subtract the first y-value from the second.

Show the worked step

7 − 3 = 4.

Step 2

Give me a hint

Divide the change in y by 3 − 1.

Show the worked step

4 ÷ 2 = 2.

SL 2.4 · Start here

Find an intercept

At a y-intercept, x = 0. At an x-intercept, y = 0.

Step 1

Give me a hint

Substitute 0 for x.

Show the worked step

2 × 0 − 6 = −6.

Step 2

Give me a hint

Solve 2x − 6 = 0.

Show the worked step

2x = 6, so x = 3.
