Question 1

Skills: Select mathematical facts and techniques · Increasing and decreasing functions

f′(x)=2x−2. On which interval is f increasing?

Show the correct answer and explanation

Correct answer: A. x > 1

A differentiable function increases where its derivative is positive.

Question 3

Skills: Select mathematical facts and techniques · Increasing and decreasing functions

Show the correct answer and explanation

Correct answer: B. 5

Substitute x=2 into the derivative; the positive result indicates increasing behavior there. The result is 5.
