Question 1

Tied observations occupy ranks 2 and 3. What rank should each receive? Choose the closest value.

Show the correct answer and explanation

Correct answer: A. 2.5

Assign each tied observation the mean of the ranks it occupies. The result is 2.5.

Question 2

Three tied observations occupy ranks 2, 3, 4. What rank should each receive? Choose the closest value.

Show the correct answer and explanation

Correct answer: B. 3

Average the three occupied ranks. The result is 3.

Question 3

For 5 distinct paired observations, the y-ranks exactly match the x-ranks. What is Spearman’s correlation?

Show the correct answer and explanation

Correct answer: D. 1

Identical rank order gives perfect positive rank correlation.

Question 4

For 5 distinct paired observations, the y-ranks are exactly the reverse of the x-ranks. What is Spearman’s correlation?

Show the correct answer and explanation

Correct answer: C. −1

Reversed ranks give perfect negative rank correlation.
