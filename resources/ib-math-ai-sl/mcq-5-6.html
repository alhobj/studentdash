Question 1

f(x)=x²−2x+3. Find the x-coordinate of its stationary point. Choose the closest value.

Show the correct answer and explanation

Correct answer: B. 1

Set f′(x)=2x−2i equal to zero. The result is 1.

Question 2

f′ changes from negative to positive at x=1. What type of stationary point is there?

Show the correct answer and explanation

Correct answer: D. Local minimum

The function changes from decreasing to increasing.

Question 3
