Booklet help: formulas and data

Related references for this section. Use values and assumptions stated in the question.

Compounding

FV = PV(1 + r/(100k))ᵏⁿ

PV is the starting amount; r is the annual percentage rate, k the periods per year and n the years. This formula is for a lump sum; regular deposits or repayments need a cash-flow method or financial solver.

Geometric patterns

uₙ = u₁rⁿ⁻¹; Sₙ = u₁(1 − rⁿ)/(1 − r)

Here r multiplies each term to give the next. If r = 1, the total is simply n times the first term.

Question 1

Skills: Select mathematical facts and techniques · Loans and annuities

A loan balance is 3000. Monthly interest is 1%, then a payment of 200 is made. Find the new balance. Choose the closest value.

Show the correct answer and explanation

Correct answer: C. 2830

Apply interest before subtracting the end-of-month payment. The result is 2830.

Question 2

Skills: Select mathematical facts and techniques · Loans and annuities

A savings account receives 300 at the end of each year for 3 years, at 5% annual interest. Find the balance just after the third deposit. Choose the closest value.

Show the correct answer and explanation

Correct answer: B. 945.75

The first deposit earns two years of interest, the second one year and the third none. The result is 945.75.

Question 3

Skills: Select mathematical facts and techniques · Loans and annuities

What equal annual payment repays a loan of 3000 in 2 years at 10% annual interest, with payments at year-end? Choose the closest value.

Show the correct answer and explanation

Correct answer: B. 1728.5714

After two payments the balance is PV(1.1)² − A(1.1+1) = 0. The result is 1728.5714.
