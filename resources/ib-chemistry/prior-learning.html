Prior learning: small steps

Take one small step at a time. Read the reminder, try the question and open a hint whenever you need one. Use a calculator if helpful. For calculations, enter a number only; units are given in the question. For word answers, use the short term requested. There is no timer or score, and answers are not saved.

Prior learning

Read element symbols

An element symbol identifies an element. Upper- and lower-case letters matter: Co is cobalt, while CO is carbon monoxide.

Step 1

Give me a hint

Read the symbols H and O.

Show the worked step

H is hydrogen and O is oxygen: two elements.

Step 2

Give me a hint

Read C and O; the small 2 is not another element.

Show the worked step

Carbon and oxygen are two elements.

Prior learning

Count atoms in a formula

A subscript applies to the symbol immediately before it. No subscript means one.

Step 1

Give me a hint

Read the small number after H.

Show the worked step

H₂ means two hydrogen atoms.

Step 2

Give me a hint

Add the hydrogen and oxygen atoms.

Show the worked step

2 hydrogen + 1 oxygen = 3 atoms.

Prior learning

Count groups and molecules

A number outside brackets multiplies everything inside. A coefficient multiplies the whole formula.

Step 1

Give me a hint

There are two OH groups.

Show the worked step

Each OH has one O; two groups contain two O atoms.

Step 2

Give me a hint

Each water molecule contains two H atoms.

Show the worked step

3 × 2 = 6 hydrogen atoms.

Prior learning

Positive and negative charges

A proton has charge +1 and an electron −1. Add the charges to find the net charge.

Step 1

Give me a hint

Calculate 3 − 3.

Show the worked step

The charges cancel: net charge 0.

Step 2

Give me a hint

Calculate 3 − 2.

Show the worked step

Net charge +1. Losing an electron makes a particle more positive.

Prior learning

Recognise states of matter

(s) means solid, (l) liquid, (g) gas and (aq) dissolved in water.

Step 1

Give me a hint

Use the first letter as a clue.

Show the worked step

(g) means gas.

Step 2

Give me a hint

Aqueous means a solution in water.

Show the worked step

(aq) means dissolved in water; it is not the same as a pure liquid.

Prior learning

Separate physical and chemical changes

In a physical change the substances keep their chemical identities. A chemical change produces different substances.

Step 1

Give me a hint

Both ice and liquid water are H₂O.

Show the worked step

This is a physical change: the state changes, not the chemical identity.

Step 2

Give me a hint

Magnesium oxide is a new substance.

Show the worked step

This is a chemical change.

Convert solution volumes

1 dm³ = 1000 cm³ and 1 cm³ = 1 mL. Mole calculations often need volume in dm³.

Step 1

Give me a hint

Show the worked step

250 ÷ 1000 = 0.250 dm³.

Step 2

Give me a hint

Multiply by 1000.

Show the worked step

0.50 × 1000 = 500 cm³.

Use powers of ten

10³ means 1000. Scientific notation keeps very large or small numbers manageable.

Step 1

Give me a hint

Multiply 2 by 1000.

Show the worked step

2 × 1000 = 2000.

Step 2

Give me a hint

10⁻³ = 0.001.

Show the worked step

0.004 = 4 × 0.001, so a = 4.
