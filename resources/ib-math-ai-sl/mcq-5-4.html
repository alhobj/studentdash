Question 1

Skills: Select mathematical facts and techniques · Tangents and normals

Find the tangent gradient to y=x² at x=1. Choose the closest value.

Show the correct answer and explanation

Correct answer: B. 2

The tangent gradient is y′=2x at the given x-coordinate. The result is 2.

Question 2

Skills: Select mathematical facts and techniques · Tangents and normals

Find the normal gradient to y=3x² at x=1. Choose the closest value.

Show the correct answer and explanation

Correct answer: D. -0.16666667

The tangent gradient is 2a; take its negative reciprocal. The result is -0.16666667.

Question 3

Skills: Select mathematical facts and techniques · Tangents and normals

The tangent to y=x² at x=1 is y=2x+c. Find c. Choose the closest value.

Show the correct answer and explanation

Correct answer: B. -1

The line passes through (i,i²); substitute to solve for c. The result is -1.

Question 4

Show the correct answer and explanation

Correct answer: D. 1.5
