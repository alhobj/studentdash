Give me a hint

Powers and roots

3² means 3 × 3. A square root reverses squaring for a non-negative number.

Step 1

Give me a hint

Multiply 3 by itself, not by 2.

Show the worked step

3 × 3 = 9.

Step 2

Give me a hint

Which non-negative number squares to 49?

Show the worked step

7 × 7 = 49, so √49 = 7.

Area and right triangles

Rectangle area = length × width. For a right triangle, a² + b² = c², where c is the hypotenuse.

Step 1

Give me a hint

Multiply the two lengths.

Show the worked step

4 × 3 = 12 cm².

Step 2

Give me a hint

Calculate √(3² + 4²).

Show the worked step

√(9 + 16) = √25 = 5.
