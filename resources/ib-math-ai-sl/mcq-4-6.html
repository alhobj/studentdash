Event relationships

P(A ∪ B) = P(A) + P(B) − P(A ∩ B); P(A|B) = P(A ∩ B)/P(B)

Check independence before multiplying.

Question 1

Skills: Select mathematical facts and techniques · Combined events and conditional probability

P(A)=0.5, P(B)=1/10 and P(A∩B)=1/20. Find P(A∪B). Choose the closest value.

Show the correct answer and explanation

Correct answer: A. 0.55

Use P(A∪B)=P(A)+P(B)−P(A∩B). The result is 0.55.

Question 2

Skills: Select mathematical facts and techniques · Combined events and conditional probability

A bag has 3 red and 4 blue counters. A red is removed without replacement. Find the probability the next counter is red. Choose the closest value.

Show the correct answer and explanation

Correct answer: C. 0.33333333

Both the red count and the total decrease by one. The result is 0.33333333.
