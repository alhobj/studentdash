Hands-on explorer · SL 3.2 · SL 3.3

Measure an inaccessible height

Move the observation point and angle of elevation. The model assumes level ground and adds the observer's eye height. Angles are in degrees.

Try a fixed scenario

At horizontal distance 20 m and elevation angle 45°, with eye height 1.5 m, find the total height.

Worked explanation

Height = 20 tan(45°) + 1.5 = 21.5 m. The horizontal distance is the adjacent side.

Hands-on explorer · SL 3.6

Move a service centre

Move centre B across the map. Coloured regions show which of two service centres is closer in straight-line distance. The query point is fixed at (5, 6); centre A is at (2, 5).

Try a fixed scenario

What is the distance between A(2, 5) and B(8, 5)?

Worked explanation

The points share a y-coordinate, so the distance is |8 − 2| = 6. The two-site Voronoi boundary is the perpendicular bisector.

Hands-on explorer · SL 3.1

Measure a three-dimensional solid

Compare volume and total external surface area. In a rectangular prism, inspect the space diagonal and the midpoint between opposite vertices. All lengths are in the same unit.

Try a fixed scenario

Find the distance from (0,0,0) to (3,4,12).

Worked explanation

√(3²+4²+12²) = 13. The midpoint is (1.5,2,6). Right-angled triangles also determine diagonals and slant heights of solids.

Hands-on explorer · SL 3.2

Solve a non-right triangle

Move two sides and their included angle. Compare the cosine-rule third side, sine-rule angles and area. Also inspect a fixed SSA example with two possible triangles.

Try a fixed scenario

Two sides are 6 and 8 with included angle 30°. Find the area.

Worked explanation

Area = ½ × 6 × 8 × sin30° = 12. In SSA data, check the supplementary sine-rule angle as well as the acute solution.

Hands-on explorer · SL 3.3

Navigate using a bearing

A bearing is measured clockwise from north. Move a journey length and bearing to resolve east and north displacement and find the reverse bearing.

Try a fixed scenario

What is the reverse bearing of 070°?

Worked explanation

Add 180° and reduce modulo 360°: 070° → 250°. Draw north lines and label your triangle; a bearing is not an angle measured from the east axis.

Hands-on explorer · SL 3.4

Open a circular sector

Change a radius and central angle in degrees. Compare arc length, area and the perimeter of the sector (arc plus two radii).

Try a fixed scenario

Find the arc length of a 90° sector of radius 4. Give a decimal answer.

Worked explanation

(90/360) × 2π × 4 = 2π ≈ 6.28319. Sector area uses the same angular fraction of πr².

Hands-on explorer · SL 2.1 · SL 3.5

Construct a perpendicular bisector

Move B while A stays at (0,0). Display the midpoint and the perpendicular bisector in standard form, which also handles vertical boundaries.

Try a fixed scenario

The points are (0,0) and (6,0). Find the x-coordinate of every point on their perpendicular bisector.

Worked explanation

The midpoint is (3,0). The joining segment is horizontal, so the perpendicular bisector is the vertical line x=3.

Hands-on explorer · SL 3.6

Add a site and measure Voronoi cells

Explore exact clipped Voronoi regions inside a 10 by 10 map. Add or remove site C and move it. Inspect the areas and a vertex shared by three regions. A=(2,2), B=(8,2); C moves above them.

Try a fixed scenario

Two service sites at (2,2) and (8,2) serve a square map 0≤x≤10, 0≤y≤10. What is the area nearest A?

Worked explanation

The boundary is x=5, so the left rectangle has area 5 × 10 = 50. With additional sites, clip each region by its pairwise bisectors.
