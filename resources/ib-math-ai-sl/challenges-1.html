1.1 · Harder task

A microscopic coating

A fictional coating contains 3.2 × 10⁹ particles. Each has mass 7.5 × 10⁻¹⁴ g. Only 80% of the supplied material reaches the surface.

Part 1

Hint

Multiply coefficients and add exponents.

Worked solution

(3.2 × 7.5) × 10⁻⁵ = 2.4 × 10⁻⁴ g.

Part 2

Hint

The deposit is 0.80 of the supplied mass.

Worked solution

2.4 × 10⁻⁴ ÷ 0.80 = 3.0 × 10⁻⁴ g.

Explain and evaluate

Compare your reasoning

The loss is a percentage of the supplied mass, not of the deposit. Multiplying by 1.20 would supply only 2.88 × 10⁻⁴ g and deposit 2.304 × 10⁻⁴ g.

1.2 · Harder task

Recover a saving plan

Weekly deposits form an arithmetic sequence. The fifth deposit is 38 and the twelfth is 73.

Part 1

Hint

Find the common difference using the seven-step gap.

Worked solution

d = (73−38)/7 = 5; u₁ = 38−4×5 = 18.

Part 2

Hint

Solve the sum inequality, then check neighbouring integers.

Worked solution

Sₙ = n(5n+31)/2. S₁₇ = 986 and S₁₈ = 1089, so 18 weeks.

Explain and evaluate

Compare your reasoning

Deposits occur at whole weeks. A fractional week has not earned the next deposit. Check the adjacent totals rather than rounding to the nearest integer.

1.3 · Harder task

Finite geometric budget

A display uses 120 lamps in its first row and 20% more in each successive row. Treat lamp counts as a continuous planning model until the final design.

Part 1

Hint

Use a finite geometric sum.

Worked solution

S₆ = 120(1.2⁶−1)/0.2 = 1191.5904.

Part 2

Hint

Compare the totals for consecutive row counts.

Worked solution

S₈ = 1979.890176; S₉ = 2495.8682112. Thus 8 rows.

Explain and evaluate

Compare your reasoning

Rows require integer counts. Rounding each row, or repeatedly rounding a recurrence, changes the total; state and apply a rounding policy before ordering.

1.4 · Harder task

Nominal gain versus purchasing power

An investment of 5000 earns 6% nominal annual interest compounded monthly for 4 years. Annual inflation is 2.5%; ignore fees and tax.

Part 1

Hint

Monthly interest is 0.06/12.

Worked solution

5000(1+0.06/12)⁴⁸ ≈ 6352.45.

Part 2

Hint

Deflate the final balance over all four years.

Worked solution

Divide the nominal balance by 1.025⁴, giving approximately 5755.00.

Explain and evaluate

Compare your reasoning

No. First obtain the effective annual nominal growth factor 1.005¹²; divide by 1.025 and subtract 1. Simple subtraction is only an approximation.

1.5 · Harder task

Solve for a decay time

A sensor reading follows R = 80e^(−0.15t), with t in hours.

Part 1

Hint

Isolate the exponential before applying ln.

Worked solution

e^(−0.15t) = 1/4; t = ln(4)/0.15 ≈ 9.24196.

Part 2

Hint

Set the ratio R/80 to 1/2.

Worked solution

t = ln(2)/0.15 ≈ 4.62098 hours.

Explain and evaluate

Compare your reasoning

An exponential model has a constant proportional decay factor; both transitions multiply the reading by 1/2, so they take the same time.

1.6 · Harder task

Bounds for a calculated density

A block has mass 84 g, rounded to the nearest gram, and volume 12.0 cm³, rounded to the nearest 0.1 cm³. Density is mass divided by volume.

Part 1

Hint

Use the smallest numerator and largest limiting denominator.

Worked solution

83.5/12.05 ≈ 6.92946 g cm⁻³.

Part 2

Hint

Reverse the choices for the upper limit.

Worked solution

84.5/11.95 ≈ 7.07113 g cm⁻³.

Explain and evaluate

Compare your reasoning

83.5≤m<84.5 and 11.95≤V<12.05. The lower density requires excluded V=12.05; the upper requires excluded m=84.5. Therefore density lies strictly between the two limits.

1.7 · Harder task

A loan with a final smaller payment

Borrow 10000 at 6% nominal annual interest compounded monthly. Pay 300 at each month-end after interest. No fees.

Part 1

Hint

Subtract the accumulated value of the payments.

Worked solution

B₁₂ = 10000(1.005)¹² − 300[(1.005¹²−1)/0.005] ≈ 6916.11.

Part 2

Hint

Find B₃₆, then add one month of interest.

Worked solution

Final payment = 1.005B₃₆ ≈ 166.80. It is less than the regular 300.

Explain and evaluate

Compare your reasoning

Once principal and accrued interest are cleared, no further debt remains. The final payment must be reduced to the outstanding amount rather than producing a fictitious credit balance.

1.8 · Harder task

Fit a quadratic from constraints

A quadratic y=ax²+bx+c passes through (0,3), (1,0) and (3,0).

Part 1

Hint

Substitute each point to obtain three equations.

Worked solution

c=3; a+b=−3; 9a+3b=−3. Subtracting 3a+3b=−9 gives 6a=6, so a=1.

Part 2

Hint

First find b, then solve the resulting quadratic.

Worked solution

b=−4; x²−4x+3=8 gives x²−4x−5=0, with roots −1 and 5.

Explain and evaluate

Compare your reasoning

At x=−1 and 5, y=8. A window restricted to x≥0 would omit the negative intersection, although it remains a valid algebraic solution.
