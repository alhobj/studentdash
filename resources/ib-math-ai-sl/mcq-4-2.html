Question 1

The first three class frequencies are 3, 4 and 5. Find the cumulative frequency through class 3. Choose the closest value.

Show the correct answer and explanation

Correct answer: A. 12

Add the frequencies up to and including the third class. The result is 12.

Question 2

A histogram class has width 2 and frequency 10. Find its frequency density. Choose the closest value.

Show the correct answer and explanation

Correct answer: D. 5

Frequency density = frequency/class width. The result is 5.

Question 3

A distribution has Q₁=11 and Q₃=23. Find its interquartile range. Choose the closest value.

Show the correct answer and explanation

Correct answer: D. 12

IQR = Q₃−Q₁. The result is 12.

Question 4

A box plot has Q₁=1 and Q₃=9. Find the upper outlier fence using 1.5×IQR. Choose the closest value.

Show the correct answer and explanation

Correct answer: A. 21

Upper fence = Q₃ + 1.5(Q₃−Q₁). The result is 21.
