R2.1 · Start here

Read a balanced equation

In 2H₂ + O₂ → 2H₂O, the coefficients show particle or mole ratios. Balance by changing coefficients, not chemical formulas.

Booklet help: formulas and data

Related references for this section. Use values and assumptions stated in the question.

Amounts and solutions

n = m/M; n = cV

Use g with g mol⁻¹, and dm³ with mol dm⁻³. For particles, multiply moles by the Avogadro constant.

Atom economy

atom economy = desired-product mass / total-reactant mass × 100%

Calculate both masses for the balanced reaction coefficients. This theoretical measure differs from experimental percentage yield.

Atomic data

Use relative atomic masses.

Units and conditions

T/K = T/°C + 273.15; 1 dm³ = 1000 cm³

Distinguish STP from SATP.

R2.2 · Start here

Calculate an average rate

Average rate of gas production = change in gas volume ÷ time interval.

Booklet help: formulas and data

Related references for this section. Use values and assumptions stated in the question.

Temperature and rate (AHL)

ln k = ln A − Eₐ/(RT)

Use natural logarithms and kelvin; express Eₐ in J mol⁻¹ with R in J K⁻¹ mol⁻¹.

Constants

Nₐ ≈ 6.02 × 10²³ mol⁻¹; R ≈ 8.31 J K⁻¹ mol⁻¹

Match the required precision.

Step 1

Skills: Arithmetic and algebra · Rates from tables

Give me a hint

Final volume minus initial volume.

Show the worked step

30 − 10 = 20 cm³.

Skills: Arithmetic and algebra · Rates from tables

R2.3 · Start here

Recognise dynamic equilibrium

At dynamic equilibrium in a closed system, forward and reverse rates are equal. Reactions continue and concentrations stay constant; the concentrations need not be equal.

Booklet help: formulas and data

Related references for this section. Use values and assumptions stated in the question.

Equilibrium and energy (AHL)

ΔG° = −RT ln K

This is an AHL connection. The SL equilibrium expression still comes from the balanced equation.
