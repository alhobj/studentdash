Balance ionic charges

An ionic formula has zero net charge. Use the smallest whole-number ratio of positive and negative ions.

Step 1

Give me a hint

Add +1 and −1.

Show the worked step

(+1) + (−1) = 0. Their formula is NaCl.

Step 2

Give me a hint

Each chloride ion contributes −1.

Show the worked step

Two Cl⁻ supply −2, balancing +2. The formula is MgCl₂.

Share an electron pair

A single covalent bond is a shared pair of electrons. A double bond contains two shared pairs.

Step 1

Give me a hint

A pair contains two items.

Show the worked step

One shared pair contains 2 electrons.

Step 2

Give me a hint

There are two pairs.

Show the worked step

2 × 2 = 4 shared electrons.

S2.3 · Start here

Explain a metal simply

Metallic bonding is attraction between positive metal ions and delocalized electrons. These electrons can move through the solid.

Step 1

Give me a hint

Look for particles that are free to move through the structure.

Show the worked step

Delocalized electrons carry charge through the solid metal; protons remain in nuclei.

Step 2

Give me a hint

They have contributed electrons to the delocalized system.

Show the worked step

The metal ions are positive.

An alloy combines a metal with other elements. A polymer contains many linked repeating units.

Step 1

Show the worked step

Brass is an alloy.
