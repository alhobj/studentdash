Question 1

Use one trapezoid of width 2, with endpoint heights 2 and 6, to estimate area. Choose the closest value.

Show the correct answer and explanation

Correct answer: D. 8

Use h(y₀+y₁)/2. The result is 8.

Question 2

Use two trapezoids, each width 1, with heights 1, 3 and 5 to estimate area. Choose the closest value.

Show the correct answer and explanation

Correct answer: B. 6

Use h[y₀+2y₁+y₂]/2. The result is 6.

Question 3

An interval from 0 to 6 is divided into 3 equal trapezoids. Find their common width. Choose the closest value.

Show the correct answer and explanation

Correct answer: A. 2

Step width equals total interval length divided by the number of subintervals. The result is 2.
