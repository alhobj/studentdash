R2.1 · Harder task

Limiting reagent and percentage yield

React 5.40 g Al with 12.8 g O₂ to form Al₂O₃: 4Al+3O₂→2Al₂O₃. Use M(Al)=27.0, M(O₂)=32.0, M(Al₂O₃)=102 g mol⁻¹. Actual dry product is 8.16 g.

Part 1

Hint

Compare n divided by the stoichiometric coefficient for each reactant.

Worked solution

n(Al)=0.200, n(O₂)=0.400 mol. Al is limiting; n(product)=0.100 mol, so mass=10.2 g.

Part 2

Hint

Use the 4:3 reactant ratio.

Worked solution

O₂ consumed=0.200×3/4=0.150 mol; remaining=0.250 mol=8.00 g.

Explain and evaluate

Compare your reasoning

Yield=8.16/10.2×100=80.0%. Losing product during collection lowers recovered yield but does not change which reactant was limiting according to the supplied starting amounts. Incomplete reaction would also affect leftover reactants.

R2.2 · Harder task

Rate and a changing slope

Gas volumes at times 0,10,20,30 s are 0,24,40,48 cm³. Conditions and collection method are unchanged.

Part 1

Hint

Use the total volume change over the whole interval.

Worked solution

48/30=1.60 cm³ s⁻¹.

Part 2

Hint

Use only the change during the final interval.

Worked solution

(48−40)/(30−20)=0.800 cm³ s⁻¹.

Explain and evaluate

Compare your reasoning

An interval average is a chord slope, while an instantaneous rate is a tangent slope. Decreasing reactant concentration can reduce successful collision frequency. More measurements near 30 s are needed to estimate the tangent; gas collection delays may also influence the data.

R2.3 · Harder task

Equilibrium composition after a disturbance

For A(g) ⇌ B(g), a closed 1.00 dm³ vessel initially contains 0.80 mol A and 0.20 mol B at equilibrium. At fixed temperature, add 0.20 mol B without changing volume.

Part 1

Hint

For the 1:1 reaction, Kc=[B]/[A].

Worked solution

Kc=0.20/0.80=0.25.

Part 2

Hint

Total amount is now 1.20 mol; solve B/A=0.25 with A+B=1.20.

Worked solution

B=0.25(1.20−B), so 1.25B=0.30 and B=0.24 mol.

Explain and evaluate

Compare your reasoning

Immediately Q=0.40/0.80=0.50>Kc, so there is net reverse reaction. At the same temperature, Kc is unchanged by the composition disturbance; the system changes composition to restore Q=Kc.
