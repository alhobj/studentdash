Question 1

Skills: Select mathematical facts and techniques · Probability and expected frequency

A bag contains 3 red and 4 blue counters. Find P(red) on one random draw. Choose the closest value.

Show the correct answer and explanation

Correct answer: A. 0.42857143

Divide favourable outcomes by the total number of counters. The result is 0.42857143.

Question 2

Skills: Select mathematical facts and techniques · Probability and expected frequency

An event has probability 1/10. In 200 independent trials, what is its expected frequency? Choose the closest value.

Show the correct answer and explanation

Correct answer: D. 20

Expected frequency = number of trials × event probability. The result is 20.
