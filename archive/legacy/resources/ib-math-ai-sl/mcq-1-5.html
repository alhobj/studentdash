Question 1

Skills: Select mathematical facts and techniques · Integer powers and logarithms

Find log₁₀(10). Choose the closest value.

Show the correct answer and explanation

Correct answer: A. 1

A logarithm is the exponent: 10 raised to this value equals the argument. The result is 1.
