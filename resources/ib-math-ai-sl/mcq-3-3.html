Question 1

The bearing of B from A is 30°. Find the bearing of A from B in degrees. Choose the closest value.

Show the correct answer and explanation

Correct answer: B. 210

Reverse a bearing by adding 180° and reducing modulo 360°. The result is 210.

Question 2

A person walks 6 km on a bearing of 30°. Find the eastward displacement in km. Choose the closest value.

Show the correct answer and explanation

Correct answer: A. 3

Eastward displacement is distance × sin(bearing). The result is 3.

Question 3

A tower is 30 m away horizontally. Its top has elevation 45°. Ignoring eye height, find its height in m. Choose the closest value.

Show the correct answer and explanation

Correct answer: C. 30

Height = horizontal distance × tan45°. The result is 30.

Question 4

A ship sails 3 km north and then 3 km east. Find its straight-line distance from the start in km. Choose the closest value.

Show the correct answer and explanation

Correct answer: A. 4.2426407

The displacements are perpendicular, so apply Pythagoras. The result is 4.2426407.
