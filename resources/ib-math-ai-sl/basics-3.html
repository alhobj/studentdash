SL 3.1 · Start here

Build a cuboid

Cuboid volume = length × width × height. Volume has cubic units.

Booklet help: formulas and data

Related references for this section. Use values and assumptions stated in the question.

Areas and volumes

A(circle) = πr²; V(cylinder) = πr²h

Keep length units consistent.

Coordinate geometry

d = √((x₂ − x₁)² + (y₂ − y₁)²)

Average coordinates for midpoints.

Three-dimensional geometry

V(cone) = πr²h/3; V(sphere) = 4πr³/3

Distinguish height from slant length.

Step 1

Give me a hint

Multiply 3 by 4.

Show the worked step

3 × 4 = 12 cm².

Step 2

Give me a hint

Multiply the base area by the height.

Show the worked step

12 × 2 = 24 cm³.

SL 3.5 · Start here

Find a midpoint and bisector

A midpoint is halfway between two points. A perpendicular bisector passes through it at a right angle.

Booklet help: formulas and data

Related references for this section. Use values and assumptions stated in the question.

Coordinate geometry

d = √((x₂ − x₁)² + (y₂ − y₁)²)

Average coordinates for midpoints.

Straight lines

m = (y₂ − y₁)/(x₂ − x₁); y − y₁ = m(x − x₁)

Vertical lines need separate treatment.

Step 1

Give me a hint

Average the x-coordinates.

Show the worked step

(2 + 8) ÷ 2 = 5.

Step 2

Give me a hint

The segment is horizontal, so its bisector is vertical through the midpoint.

Show the worked step

The equation is x = 5.

Find the nearer site

A Voronoi region contains points nearer its site than any other site.

Booklet help: formulas and data

Related references for this section. Use values and assumptions stated in the question.

Coordinate geometry

d = √((x₂ − x₁)² + (y₂ − y₁)²)

Average coordinates for midpoints.

Straight lines

m = (y₂ − y₁)/(x₂ − x₁); y − y₁ = m(x − x₁)

Vertical lines need separate treatment.

Step 1

Give me a hint

All points lie on the same horizontal line.

Show the worked step

Distance = 3 − 0 = 3.

Step 2

Give me a hint

Subtract 3 from 10; compare the two distances.

Show the worked step

10 − 3 = 7. Since 3 < 7, the point is in A’s region.
