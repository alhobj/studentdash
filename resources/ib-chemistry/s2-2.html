Your challenge: Build CH₄, NH₃ and H₂O. Compare the electron-domain arrangement with the molecular shape as bonds are replaced by lone pairs.

Model, method and limits

Four electron domains have an approximately tetrahedral arrangement. Four bonds give tetrahedral CH₄, three bonds plus one lone pair give trigonal pyramidal NH₃, and two bonds plus two lone pairs give bent H₂O. The buttons are a schematic projection, not measured bond angles.

Compare the three bonding models
  • Ionic: electrostatic attraction between oppositely charged ions throughout a lattice. Solid NaCl has fixed ions; molten NaCl has mobile ions.
  • Covalent: electrostatic attraction between a shared pair of electrons and both bonded nuclei. Water contains molecules; diamond and graphite form extended covalent structures.
  • Metallic: electrostatic attraction between positive metal ions and delocalized electrons. Mobile electrons allow conduction in solid and molten copper.

Melting ice overcomes intermolecular attractions; it does not break the O–H covalent bonds within each water molecule. Graphite conducts along its layers because some electrons are delocalized. Diamond has no comparable mobile charge carriers. Pure water has very low conductivity due to its slight self-ionization.

Explain: Why is “all covalent substances have low melting points and cannot conduct” incorrect? Use diamond and graphite as counterexamples.

Separate bond polarity from molecular polarity

Use electron domains and symmetry to predict molecular shape and whether bond dipoles cancel. Multiple bonds count as one electron domain in VSEPR.

Choose a scenario, enter a prediction and check it. Try again or reveal the explanation, then move to another scenario.

Scenarios and worked explanations

Carbon dioxide

CO₂ has two bonding domains and no lone pairs on its central carbon. Which description is correct?

Answer: Linear and non-polar overall. The two polar C=O bonds point in opposite directions in a linear molecule, so their equal dipoles cancel.

Water

H₂O has two bonding pairs and two lone pairs around oxygen. What is its molecular shape and polarity?

Answer: Bent and polar. Four electron domains have a tetrahedral arrangement, but the molecular shape describes atom positions: bent. The O–H dipoles do not cancel.

Ammonia

NH₃ has three bonding pairs and one lone pair around nitrogen. What is its molecular shape?

Answer: Trigonal pyramidal. Four electron domains surround nitrogen. One is a lone pair, leaving three bonds arranged in a trigonal pyramidal molecular shape.

20 multiple-choice questions

Click A, B, C or D for immediate feedback. You can change your answer or open the explanation. These independently authored questions include related examples for repeated practice.

Question 1

What holds the two hydrogen atoms together in an H₂ molecule?

Show the correct answer and explanation

Correct answer: D. Attraction between the shared electron pair and both positive nuclei

A covalent bond involves a shared electron pair attracted to the positively charged nuclei of both atoms.

Question 2

How many electrons are shared in the N≡N bond of nitrogen gas?

Show the correct answer and explanation

Correct answer: C. 6

A triple bond contains three shared electron pairs, hence six bonding electrons.

Question 3

For bonds between the same pair of elements, how does a triple bond generally compare with a single bond?

Show the correct answer and explanation

Correct answer: A. It is shorter and stronger.

Greater bond order generally increases bond strength and decreases bond length for the same bonded elements.

Question 4

In the usual Lewis formula O=C=O, how many lone pairs are present on each oxygen atom?

Show the correct answer and explanation

Correct answer: C. 2

Each oxygen has two bonding pairs in its double bond and two lone pairs, completing an octet.

Question 5

In the Lewis formula of hydrogen peroxide, H–O–O–H, how many lone pairs are present in the whole molecule?

Show the correct answer and explanation

Correct answer: B. 4

Each oxygen forms two single bonds and has two lone pairs, giving four lone pairs in total.

Question 6

Ammonia donates its nitrogen lone pair to H⁺ to form NH₄⁺. Where do both electrons in the newly formed bond originally come from?

Show the correct answer and explanation

Correct answer: D. The nitrogen atom of ammonia

In a coordination bond, both electrons of the shared pair originate from the donor atom.

Question 7

A central atom has three bonding domains and no lone pairs. What molecular geometry does the VSEPR model predict?

Show the correct answer and explanation

Correct answer: D. Trigonal planar

Three electron domains arrange in a plane approximately 120° apart; with no lone pairs, the molecular geometry is trigonal planar.

Question 8

A central atom has two bonding domains and two lone pairs. What are its electron-domain geometry and molecular geometry, respectively?

Show the correct answer and explanation

Correct answer: A. Tetrahedral and bent

Four electron domains give tetrahedral electron-domain geometry; the two bonded atoms form a bent molecular shape.

Question 9

Why is the H–O–H angle in water smaller than the ideal tetrahedral angle?

Show the correct answer and explanation

Correct answer: D. Lone pairs repel bonding domains more strongly than bonding domains repel one another.

Water has two lone pairs whose stronger repulsion compresses the angle between its bonding domains.

Question 10

Electronegativities are H = 2.2, C = 2.6, N = 3.0 and O = 3.4. Which of these bonds is most polar?

Show the correct answer and explanation

Correct answer: D. O–H

The electronegativity differences are 1.2, 0.4, 0.8 and 0.4 respectively; O–H has the largest difference.

Question 11

Why is a carbon dioxide molecule non-polar even though each C=O bond is polar?

Show the correct answer and explanation

Correct answer: B. The equal bond dipoles point in opposite directions and cancel in its linear geometry.

Molecular polarity depends on the vector sum of bond dipoles; the two equal dipoles cancel in linear CO₂.

Question 12

Which molecule has a net dipole moment?

Show the correct answer and explanation

Correct answer: B. CH₃Cl

The bond dipoles in CH₃Cl do not cancel. The other listed molecules have symmetric geometries that cancel their bond dipoles.

Question 13

Which intermolecular force is present between molecules of every molecular substance, including non-polar substances?

Show the correct answer and explanation

Correct answer: A. London dispersion forces

Fluctuations in electron distributions create instantaneous and induced dipoles, giving London forces in all molecular substances.

Question 14

Which pure liquid can form hydrogen bonds between its own molecules?

Show the correct answer and explanation

Correct answer: B. CH₃CH₂OH

Ethanol has an O–H group and oxygen lone pairs. The other substances lack a hydrogen bonded directly to N, O or F.

Question 15

Ethanol and dimethyl ether have the same molecular formula, C₂H₆O. Why does ethanol have the higher boiling point?

Show the correct answer and explanation

Correct answer: A. Ethanol molecules can hydrogen-bond to one another, whereas dimethyl ether molecules cannot do so with one another.

The O–H group permits intermolecular hydrogen bonding in ethanol; dimethyl ether has no suitable donor hydrogen.

Question 16

Which alcohol is expected to be the most soluble in water?

Show the correct answer and explanation

Correct answer: D. Methanol, CH₃OH

All have an –OH group, but methanol has the smallest non-polar hydrocarbon part and is most compatible with water.

Question 17

Why does diamond have a very high melting temperature?

Show the correct answer and explanation

Correct answer: A. Strong covalent bonds extend throughout its three-dimensional network.

Changing the solid network requires overcoming strong covalent bonding, rather than only weak intermolecular forces.

Question 18

Why can graphite conduct electricity along its layers?

Show the correct answer and explanation

Correct answer: D. Some electrons are delocalized and can move through the layers.

Graphite has mobile delocalized electrons that can transport charge along its layers.

Question 19

Which comparison of silicon dioxide and sulfur dioxide is correct?

Show the correct answer and explanation

Correct answer: A. Solid SiO₂ has a covalent network, whereas SO₂ consists of separate molecules.

Silicon dioxide is an extended covalent network; sulfur dioxide is a molecular substance with intermolecular attractions.

Question 20

A non-polar molecule approaches a polar molecule. Which interaction can result when the polar molecule distorts the other's electron distribution?

Show the correct answer and explanation

Correct answer: A. Dipole-induced dipole attraction

A permanent dipole can induce a dipole in a neighbouring non-polar molecule, producing an attractive interaction.
