S3.1 · Harder task

Read a jump in ionization energy

A fictional period-3 element has successive ionization energies 740,1450,7730,10500 kJ mol⁻¹.

Part 1

Hint

Find the jump between removing outer-shell and inner-shell electrons.

Worked solution

The large jump after IE₂ suggests two outer-shell electrons.

Part 2

Hint

Add IE₁ and IE₂, then multiply by amount.

Worked solution

0.50(740+1450)=1095 kJ.

Explain and evaluate

Compare your reasoning

Group 2 is indicated. The third electron is removed from an inner shell, closer to the nucleus with less shielding and stronger attraction. The first two are more weakly held outer-shell electrons.

S3.2 · Harder task

Combustion evidence and isomerism

Complete combustion of 0.300 g of a compound containing only C,H,O gives 0.440 g CO₂ and 0.180 g H₂O. Its molar mass is 60.0 g mol⁻¹. Use Ar(C)=12, H=1, O=16.

Part 1

Hint

Every CO₂ contains one carbon.

Worked solution

n(C)=0.440/44.0=0.0100 mol.

Part 2

Hint

Find H amount, then oxygen mass by difference and compare with 0.300/60.0 mol molecules.

Worked solution

n(H)=2×0.180/18=0.0200; masses C=0.120 g, H=0.0200 g, O=0.160 g, so n(O)=0.0100. Formula C₂H₄O₂.

Explain and evaluate

Compare your reasoning

Ethanoic acid, CH₃COOH, and methyl methanoate, HCOOCH₃, are carboxylic-acid and ester isomers. Combustion establishes elemental amounts, not the connectivity of the atoms.
