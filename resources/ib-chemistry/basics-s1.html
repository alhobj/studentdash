Particles and mixtures

An element contains one type of atom. A compound contains different elements chemically bonded; a mixture contains different substances together.

Step 1

Give me a hint

Both atoms in O₂ are oxygen.

Show the worked step

Only one type of atom: oxygen. O₂ is an element, even though each molecule has two atoms.

Step 2

Give me a hint

Mixing does not automatically create new chemical bonds.

Show the worked step

Salt and sand form a mixture; each substance retains its identity.

S1.2 · Start here

Count subatomic particles

Atomic number Z counts protons. Mass number A counts protons plus neutrons. A neutral atom has equal numbers of protons and electrons.

Step 1

Give me a hint

The atomic number is the proton count.

Show the worked step

It has 6 protons.

Step 2

Give me a hint

Calculate mass number minus atomic number.

Show the worked step

12 − 6 = 6 neutrons.

Place a few electrons

For these first-ten-element examples, the first shell holds up to 2 electrons and the second up to 8. This simple shell model is a starting point for orbital configurations.

Step 1

Give me a hint

Subtract the two placed electrons from the total.

Show the worked step

3 − 2 = 1 electron in the second shell.

Step 2

Give me a hint

Put 2 in the first shell, then count what remains.

Show the worked step

10 − 2 = 8. Neon has a full second shell.

S1.4 · Start here

Mass to moles

Amount n = mass m ÷ molar mass M. M is measured in g mol⁻¹; n is measured in mol.

Step 1

Give me a hint

Use n = 18 ÷ 18.

Show the worked step

18 ÷ 18 = 1 mol.

Step 2

Give me a hint

Use the same molar mass.

Show the worked step

36 ÷ 18 = 2 mol.

S1.5 · Start here

Temperature and gas volume

Gas-law temperatures must be in kelvin: T/K = temperature/°C + 273.15. At constant pressure and amount, ideal-gas volume is proportional to kelvin temperature.

Step 1

Give me a hint

Add 273.15.

Show the worked step

0 + 273.15 = 273.15 K.

Step 2

Give me a hint

The kelvin temperature doubles, so the volume doubles.

Show the worked step

2 × (600 ÷ 300) = 4 dm³.
