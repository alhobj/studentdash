SL 5.6 · Start here

Locate a stationary point

At a stationary point the derivative is zero. Its sign on either side tells you more.

Step 1

Give me a hint

Solve 2x − 4 = 0.

Show the worked step

2x = 4, so x = 2.

Step 2

Give me a hint

Substitute 3 into 2x − 4.

Show the worked step

6 − 4 = 2. At x = 1 it is −2, so the sign changes from negative to positive: a minimum.

SL 5.8 · Start here

One trapezoid

Trapezoid area = width × (first height + second height) ÷ 2.

Step 1

Give me a hint

Add the heights and divide by 2.

Show the worked step

(2 + 6) ÷ 2 = 4.

Step 2

Give me a hint

Multiply the width by the average height.

Show the worked step

3 × 4 = 12.
