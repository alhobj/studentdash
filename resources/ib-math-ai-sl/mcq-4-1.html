Click A, B, C or D for immediate feedback. You can change your answer or open the explanation. These independently authored questions include related examples for repeated practice.

Question 1

A survey samples 20 people from a population of 200. What percentage is sampled? Choose the closest value.

Show the correct answer and explanation

Correct answer: D. 10

Sample percentage = sample size/population size × 100. The result is 10.

Question 2

A population of 1000 includes 100 members of group A. A proportional stratified sample has size 80. How many should come from A? Choose the closest value.

Show the correct answer and explanation

Correct answer: C. 8

Allocate sample places in the same proportion as the population. The result is 8.

Question 3

A school surveys only the first 11 volunteers who respond online. What is a key concern?

Show the correct answer and explanation

Correct answer: D. Volunteer bias

Willingness to respond may be related to the topic; a volunteer sample need not be representative.

Question 4

A list has 100 people. A systematic sample of 10 is needed. What sampling interval should be used after a random start? Choose the closest value.

Show the correct answer and explanation

Correct answer: B. 10

Divide population size by sample size. The result is 10.
