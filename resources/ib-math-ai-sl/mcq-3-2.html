Question 1

Skills: Select mathematical facts and techniques · Right and non-right triangles

A right triangle has hypotenuse 6 cm and an angle of 30°. Find the side opposite that angle, in cm. Choose the closest value.

Show the correct answer and explanation

Correct answer: C. 3

Opposite = hypotenuse × sin 30°. The result is 3.
