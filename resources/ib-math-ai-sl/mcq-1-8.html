Question 1

Solve x + y = 5 and x − y = 1. Find x. Choose the closest value.

Show the correct answer and explanation

Correct answer: D. 3

Add the equations to eliminate y, then divide by 2. The result is 3.

Question 2

If x = 3, solve 2x + 3y = 12 for y. Choose the closest value.

Show the correct answer and explanation

Correct answer: C. 2

Substitute x, subtract 2x, and divide by 3. The result is 2.

Question 3

Which pair gives the roots of x² − 5x + 6 = 0?

Show the correct answer and explanation

Correct answer: A. 2 and 3

The expression factors as (x−2)(x−3).

Question 4

A system has x+y+z=7, x−y=1, and z=2. Find y. Choose the closest value.

Show the correct answer and explanation

Correct answer: D. 2

Substitute z=2, then subtract the second equation from x+y to get 2y. The result is 2.
