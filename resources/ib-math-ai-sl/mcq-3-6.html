Question 1

Two sites are at (0,0) and (6,0). On their Voronoi boundary, x=k. Find k. Choose the closest value.

Show the correct answer and explanation

Correct answer: C. 3

The boundary is the perpendicular bisector of the segment joining the sites. The result is 3.

Question 2

Sites A=(0,0), B=(12,0), C=(0,12). Which is nearest to P=(3,0)?

Show the correct answer and explanation

Correct answer: A. A

Distances to A and B are a and 3a; the distance to C is √17a, so A is nearest.

Question 3

On a horizontal road, sites are at x=0, x=6, and x=18. Find the length of the middle site’s Voronoi interval. Choose the closest value.

Show the correct answer and explanation

Correct answer: C. 9

Boundaries are at x=3 and x=12; subtract to find the interval length. The result is 9.

Question 4

Sites are A=(0,0) and B=(6,0). A point P=(3,1) lies where?

Show the correct answer and explanation

Correct answer: B. On the boundary between their regions

Its x-coordinate is the midpoint; its distances to A and B are equal.
