Read a balanced equation

In 2H₂ + O₂ → 2H₂O, the coefficients show particle or mole ratios. Balance by changing coefficients, not chemical formulas.

Step 1

Give me a hint

Two molecules, each containing two hydrogen atoms.

Show the worked step

2 × 2 = 4 hydrogen atoms.

Step 2

Give me a hint

The H₂:H₂O mole ratio is 2:2.

Show the worked step

2 mol H₂ produces 2 mol H₂O.

R2.2 · Start here

Calculate an average rate

Average rate of gas production = change in gas volume ÷ time interval.

Step 1

Give me a hint

Final volume minus initial volume.

Show the worked step

30 − 10 = 20 cm³.

Step 2

Give me a hint

Divide the increase by the time.

Show the worked step

20 ÷ 5 = 4 cm³ s⁻¹.

R2.3 · Start here

Recognise dynamic equilibrium

At dynamic equilibrium in a closed system, forward and reverse rates are equal. Reactions continue and concentrations stay constant; the concentrations need not be equal.

Step 1

Give me a hint

The two rates are equal at equilibrium.

Show the worked step

The reverse rate is also 3 mmol dm⁻³ s⁻¹.

Step 2

Give me a hint

Dynamic means that both directions are still occurring.

Show the worked step

Both reactions continue at equal rates.
