Question 1

Skills: Select mathematical facts and techniques · Optimization in context

A rectangle has perimeter 24 m. Find the side length of the maximum-area rectangle, in m. Choose the closest value.

Show the correct answer and explanation

Correct answer: C. 6

For fixed perimeter, the maximum-area rectangle is a square; divide the perimeter by 4. The result is 6.

Question 2

Skills: Select mathematical facts and techniques · Optimization in context

A rectangle is fenced on three sides against a wall using 36 m of fence. Find the optimal width perpendicular to the wall. Choose the closest value.

Show the correct answer and explanation

Correct answer: A. 9

With 2w+l=12a, area is w(12a−2w); set its derivative 12a−4w to zero. The result is 9.

Question 3

Skills: Select mathematical facts and techniques · Optimization in context

Profit is P(x)=−x²+6x−1 for x≥0. Find the output x that maximizes profit. Choose the closest value.

Show the correct answer and explanation

Correct answer: C. 3

Set P′=−2x+2a to zero; the downward-opening quadratic gives a maximum. The result is 3.
