Question 1

Find the x-coordinate of the vertex of y=(x−3)²+1. Choose the closest value.

Show the correct answer and explanation

Correct answer: A. 3

The squared term is zero at the vertex. The result is 3.

Question 2

Find the positive x-intercept of y=x²−9. Choose the closest value.

Show the correct answer and explanation

Correct answer: C. 3

Set y=0 and take the positive square root. The result is 3.
