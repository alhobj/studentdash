Question 1

Skills: Select mathematical facts and techniques · Normal models and inverse probabilities

A normal distribution has mean 51 and standard deviation 5. Find the z-score of 61. Choose the closest value.

Show the correct answer and explanation

Correct answer: B. 2

Use z=(x−μ)/σ. The result is 2.

Question 2

Skills: Select mathematical facts and techniques · Normal models and inverse probabilities

X is normal with mean 21 and standard deviation 3. Find P(X < 21). Choose the closest value.

Show the correct answer and explanation

Correct answer: C. 0.5

A normal distribution is symmetric about its mean. The result is 0.5.

Skills: Select mathematical facts and techniques · Normal models and inverse probabilities

Show the correct answer and explanation

Correct answer: D. 38.84

Transform back using x=μ+zσ. The result is 38.84.

Question 4

Skills: Select mathematical facts and techniques · Normal models and inverse probabilities

X is normal with mean 11 and standard deviation 2. Using P(−1<Z<1)≈0.6827, find P(9<X<13). Choose the closest value.

Show the correct answer and explanation

Correct answer: D. 0.6827

The endpoints are one standard deviation below and above the mean. The result is 0.6827.
