Question 1

A length is reported as 11 cm to the nearest cm. What is its lower bound, in cm? Choose the closest value.

Show the correct answer and explanation

Correct answer: B. 10.5

The rounding interval extends half a unit below and above the reported value. The result is 10.5.

Question 2

The exact value is 60; an estimate is 65. Find the absolute percentage error. Choose the closest value.

Show the correct answer and explanation

Correct answer: B. 8.3333333

Absolute percentage error is |estimate−exact|/|exact| × 100. The result is 8.3333333.

Question 4

Correct answer: A. 74.75
