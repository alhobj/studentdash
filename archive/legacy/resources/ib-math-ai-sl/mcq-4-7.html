Discrete distributions

E(X) = ΣxP(X = x); binomial mean = np, variance = np(1 − p)

For the binomial model, n counts trials and p is success probability. Trials must be independent with the same p.

Question 1

Skills: Select mathematical facts and techniques · Discrete distributions and expectation

X equals 10 with probability 0.2, otherwise 0. Find E(X). Choose the closest value.

Show the correct answer and explanation

Correct answer: C. 2

Multiply each value by its probability and sum. The result is 2.
