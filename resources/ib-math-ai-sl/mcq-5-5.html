Antiderivatives and area

∫xⁿ dx = xⁿ⁺¹/(n + 1) + C, n ≠ −1

Increase the power and divide by the new power; this rule excludes n = −1. Use a boundary condition to find C, or evaluate between limits for a definite integral.

Question 1

Evaluate ∫ from 0 to 1 of 2x dx. Choose the closest value.

Show the correct answer and explanation

Correct answer: B. 1

An antiderivative is x²; evaluate at the two endpoints. The result is 1.

Question 2

F′(x)=2x and F(0)=3. Find F(3). Choose the closest value.

Show the correct answer and explanation

Correct answer: C. 12

Integrate to F=x²+C; the initial value gives C=a. The result is 12.
