Expected value

Expected value is the sum of each outcome multiplied by its probability.

Step 1

Give me a hint

Multiply the prize by its chance.

Show the worked step

10 × 0.2 = 2.

Step 2

Give me a hint

Add the contribution from a zero prize, which is 0.

Show the worked step

10 × 0.2 + 0 × 0.8 = 2. This is a long-run average.
