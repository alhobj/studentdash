Compounding

FV = PV(1 + r/(100k))ᵏⁿ

PV is the starting amount; r is the annual percentage rate, k the periods per year and n the years. This formula is for a lump sum; regular deposits or repayments need a cash-flow method or financial solver.

Question 1

3000 is invested at 2% annually, compounded annually. Find its value after 3 years. Choose the closest value.

Show the correct answer and explanation

Correct answer: C. 3183.624

Use FV = PV(1+r)³ with r as a decimal. The result is 3183.624.

Question 2

A machine worth 6000 depreciates by 10% each year. Find its value after 2 years. Choose the closest value.

Show the correct answer and explanation

Correct answer: B. 4860

Each year retains 90% of the preceding value. The result is 4860.

Question 3

An investment grows by 6% in one year while prices rise by 2%. Find the percentage real growth. Choose the closest value.

Show the correct answer and explanation

Correct answer: D. 3.9215686

Divide the investment growth factor by the price growth factor, then subtract 1 and multiply by 100. The result is 3.9215686.

Question 4

3000 earns 6% nominal annual interest compounded monthly for one year. Find its final value. Choose the closest value.

Show the correct answer and explanation

Correct answer: C. 3185.0334

The monthly rate is 0.06/12 and there are 12 compounding periods. The result is 3185.0334.
