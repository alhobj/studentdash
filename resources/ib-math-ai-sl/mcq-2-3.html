Question 1

Skills: Select mathematical facts and techniques · Graphs and combinations

f(x)=2x and g(x)=x+3. Find (f+g)(2). Choose the closest value.

Show the correct answer and explanation

Correct answer: C. 9

Evaluate both functions at 2 and add the outputs. The result is 9.

Question 4
