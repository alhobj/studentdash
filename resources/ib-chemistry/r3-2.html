Scenarios and worked explanations

Oxidation state

In MnO₄⁻, each oxygen has oxidation state −2. What is the oxidation state of manganese?

Answer: 7. Let x be the Mn oxidation state: x + 4(−2) = −1, so x = +7.

Electron count

How many electrons are released when one Zn atom forms Zn²⁺?

Answer: 2. Zn → Zn²⁺ + 2e⁻. Loss of electrons is oxidation; charge is conserved.

Oxidizing agent

Zn + Cu²⁺ → Zn²⁺ + Cu. Which species is the oxidizing agent?

Answer: Cu²⁺. Cu²⁺ accepts electrons and is reduced. By accepting those electrons it causes zinc to be oxidized.

Scenarios and worked explanations

Anode reaction

Which half-equation occurs at the anode?

Answer: Zn → Zn²⁺ + 2e⁻. Oxidation occurs at the anode. Zinc atoms release electrons and enter solution as Zn²⁺.

External circuit

In which direction do electrons flow through the external wire?

Answer: From the zinc electrode to the copper electrode. Electrons released by zinc oxidation travel through the wire to the copper electrode, where Cu²⁺ is reduced.

Salt bridge

What is the main role of the salt bridge?

Answer: Allow ion migration to maintain charge balance. Ions migrate to prevent charge build-up in each half-cell. Electron transfer between electrodes occurs in the external circuit.
