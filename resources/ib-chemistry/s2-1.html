Model, method and limits

Total charge is the sum of each ion count multiplied by its signed charge. Al³⁺ and O²⁻ balance at two aluminium ions and three oxide ions. The counters represent a formula ratio, not isolated molecules in an ionic solid.

Connect particles to properties

Choose a substance and state, then predict whether it conducts electricity. Use the particles and their mobility to explain your answer.

Schematic only, not to scale. Positions are illustrative; the diagram does not simulate motion.

Compare the three bonding models
  • Ionic: electrostatic attraction between oppositely charged ions throughout a lattice. Solid NaCl has fixed ions; molten NaCl has mobile ions.
  • Covalent: electrostatic attraction between a shared pair of electrons and both bonded nuclei. Water contains molecules; diamond and graphite form extended covalent structures.
  • Metallic: electrostatic attraction between positive metal ions and delocalized electrons. Mobile electrons allow conduction in solid and molten copper.

Melting ice overcomes intermolecular attractions; it does not break the O–H covalent bonds within each water molecule. Graphite conducts along its layers because some electrons are delocalized. Diamond has no comparable mobile charge carriers. Pure water has very low conductivity due to its slight self-ionization.

Explain: Why is “all covalent substances have low melting points and cannot conduct” incorrect? Use diamond and graphite as counterexamples.

Build a neutral ionic formula

Balance total positive and negative charge using the simplest whole-number ratio. Keep polyatomic ions together.

Choose a scenario, enter a prediction and check it. Try again or reveal the explanation, then move to another scenario.

Scenarios and worked explanations

Magnesium chloride

Which formula combines Mg²⁺ and Cl⁻ in a neutral compound?

Answer: MgCl₂. One Mg²⁺ requires two Cl⁻ ions: +2 + 2(−1) = 0.

Aluminium oxide

Which formula combines Al³⁺ and O²⁻ in the simplest neutral ratio?

Answer: Al₂O₃. Two aluminium ions give +6 and three oxide ions give −6. The simplest ratio is 2:3.

Calcium nitrate

Which formula combines Ca²⁺ with nitrate, NO₃⁻?

Answer: Ca(NO₃)₂. Two nitrate ions balance one calcium ion. Parentheses show that the entire NO₃ group occurs twice.
