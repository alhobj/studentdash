Question 1

Skills: Select mathematical facts and techniques · Power-rule differentiation

f(x)=3x³. Find f′(2). Choose the closest value.

Show the correct answer and explanation

Correct answer: A. 36

Differentiate to get 3ax², then substitute x=2. The result is 36.
