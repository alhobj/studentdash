Your challenge: Connect all four carbon atoms to make C₄H₁₀. Then rearrange the bonds to discover its other chain isomer.

Model, method and limits

A connected four-carbon tree has three C–C bonds and formula C₄H₁₀. A straight chain is butane; one carbon bonded to the other three gives 2-methylpropane. The drawing shows connectivity, not three-dimensional geometry. Only single bonds are modelled.

S3.2 / Practice

Spot the functional group

Read condensed structural formulas and distinguish groups that contain similar atoms but different bonding arrangements.

Choose a scenario, enter a prediction and check it. Try again or reveal the explanation, then move to another scenario.

Scenarios and worked explanations

CH₃CH₂OH

Which class contains CH₃CH₂OH?

Answer: Alcohol. The hydroxyl group is attached to a saturated carbon. There is no carbonyl group and the oxygen is not bonded to two carbon groups.

CH₃COOCH₃

Which class contains CH₃C(=O)OCH₃?

Answer: Ester. An ester contains –C(=O)–O– between carbon-containing groups. A carboxylic acid would have –C(=O)–OH.

CH₃COCH₃

Which class contains CH₃C(=O)CH₃?

Answer: Ketone. The carbonyl carbon is bonded to two carbon groups. An aldehyde has a hydrogen directly attached to its carbonyl carbon.

Homologous series

What is the formula difference between successive members of the same unbranched saturated alcohol homologous series?

Answer: CH₂. Successive members differ by CH₂ and retain the same functional group. Their physical properties show trends, while many chemical properties are similar.

Distinguish formulas and isomers

Compare molecular formulas with atom connectivity. A different way of writing the same connectivity is not a new structural isomer.

Choose a scenario, enter a prediction and check it. Try again or reveal the explanation, then move to another scenario.

Scenarios and worked explanations

Same formula, different group

CH₃CH₂OH and CH₃OCH₃ both have molecular formula C₂H₆O. What is their relationship?

Answer: Structural isomers. They have the same molecular formula but different connectivity: one is an alcohol, the other an ether.

Branching

CH₃CH₂CH₂CH₃ and (CH₃)₃CH are examples of which relationship?

Answer: Chain structural isomers. Both have formula C₄H₁₀. One has an unbranched chain and the other a branched carbon skeleton.

Count carbon atoms

How many carbon atoms are in CH₃CH₂COOCH₃?

Answer: 4. Count the first CH₃, the CH₂, the carbonyl carbon in COO, and the final CH₃: four carbons in total.
