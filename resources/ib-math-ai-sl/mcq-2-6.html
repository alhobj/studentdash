Question 1

A model predicts 21 but the observation is 25. Find the residual (observed minus predicted). Choose the closest value.

Show the correct answer and explanation

Correct answer: B. 4

Residual = observed − predicted. The result is 4.

Question 2

A model y=2x+3 is fitted using x-values from 0 to 10. What does it predict at x=5? Choose the closest value.

Show the correct answer and explanation

Correct answer: A. 13

Substitute into the fitted equation; 5 is within the observed interval. The result is 13.

Question 3

A model was fitted for ages 10–21. Predicting for age 31 is an example of what?

Show the correct answer and explanation

Correct answer: D. Extrapolation

The prediction is outside the range used to fit the model and may be unreliable.

Question 4

A proposed model has residuals 1, -1, 2 and -2. Find the sum of squared residuals. Choose the closest value.

Show the correct answer and explanation

Correct answer: C. 10

Square every residual before adding; opposite signs do not cancel squared errors. The result is 10.
