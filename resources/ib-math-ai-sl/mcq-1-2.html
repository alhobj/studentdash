Arithmetic patterns

uₙ = u₁ + (n − 1)d; Sₙ = n(u₁ + uₙ)/2

Here d is the step between terms and n counts terms. Use uₙ for one term and Sₙ for a total.

Question 1

An arithmetic sequence has first term 3 and common difference 2. Find term 6. Choose the closest value.

Show the correct answer and explanation

Correct answer: C. 13

Use uₙ = u₁ + (n−1)d. The result is 13.

Question 2

Find the sum of the first 6 terms of the arithmetic sequence with u₁ = 3 and d = 2. Choose the closest value.

Show the correct answer and explanation

Correct answer: A. 48

Use Sₙ = n[2u₁ + (n−1)d]/2. The result is 48.

Question 3

Terms 3 and 8 of an arithmetic sequence are 7 and 17. Find d. Choose the closest value.

Show the correct answer and explanation

Correct answer: B. 2

There are five equal increments between terms 3 and 8; divide their difference by 5. The result is 2.
