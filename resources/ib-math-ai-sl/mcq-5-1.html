Question 1

Skills: Select mathematical facts and techniques · Derivative as a limiting rate

For f(x)=x², find the average rate of change from x=1 to x=3. Choose the closest value.

Show the correct answer and explanation

Correct answer: B. 4

Use [f(b)−f(a)]/(b−a). The result is 4.

Question 2

Skills: Select mathematical facts and techniques · Derivative as a limiting rate

For f(x)=3x², what is the limiting gradient of secants at x=1? Choose the closest value.

Show the correct answer and explanation

Correct answer: B. 6

The limiting secant gradient is f′(1); differentiating gives f′(x)=2ax. The result is 6.

Question 3

Skills: Select mathematical facts and techniques · Derivative as a limiting rate

A distance changes from 10 m at t=2 s to 34 m at t=8 s. Find the average speed over this interval in m s⁻¹. Choose the closest value.

Show the correct answer and explanation

Correct answer: B. 4

Divide the change in distance, 24 m, by the elapsed time, 6 s. The result is 4.

Question 4

Skills: Select mathematical facts and techniques · Derivative as a limiting rate

f(x)=x². Find [f(1+0.1)−f(1)]/0.1. Choose the closest value.

Show the correct answer and explanation

Correct answer: B. 2.1

Expand (x+h)²−x² and divide by h to obtain 2x+h. The result is 2.1.
