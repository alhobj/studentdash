Transfer a proton

A Brønsted–Lowry acid donates a proton (H⁺); a base accepts one.

Step 1

Give me a hint

Compare HCl with Cl⁻.

Show the worked step

Each HCl donates one H⁺.

Step 2

Give me a hint

Accepting a proton is the role of a base.

Show the worked step

Water is acting as a Brønsted–Lowry base.

R3.3 · Start here

Split a bond equally

In homolytic bond breaking, each atom receives one electron from the shared pair. A radical has an unpaired electron.

Step 1

Give me a hint

Share the two bonding electrons equally.

Show the worked step

Each chlorine atom receives one electron from the bond.

Step 2

Give me a hint

Each of the two chlorine atoms becomes a radical.

Show the worked step

Cl₂ → 2Cl•. Two chlorine radicals form.

R3.4 · Start here

Donate an electron pair

A nucleophile donates an electron pair to an electron-deficient centre. In a mechanism, a full curly arrow shows movement of a pair.

Step 1

Give me a hint

A full curly arrow moves one pair, not one electron.

Show the worked step

A full curly arrow represents movement of 2 electrons.

Step 2

Give me a hint

Use the definition above.

Show the worked step

A nucleophile donates an electron pair; an electrophile accepts it.
