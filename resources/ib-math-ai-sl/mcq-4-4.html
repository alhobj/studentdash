Question 1

A regression line is y=2x+3. Predict y at x=5. Choose the closest value.

Show the correct answer and explanation

Correct answer: D. 13

Substitute into the fitted regression equation. The result is 13.

Question 2

Skills: Select mathematical facts and techniques · Pearson correlation and regression

A regression predicts 16 for an observed value of 19. Find the residual. Choose the closest value.

Show the correct answer and explanation

Correct answer: B. 3

Residual is observed minus predicted. The result is 3.

Question 3

Skills: Select mathematical facts and techniques · Pearson correlation and regression

A sample has Pearson correlation r=−0.5. What does the negative sign indicate?

Show the correct answer and explanation

Correct answer: C. Higher x tends to accompany lower y

The sign gives the direction of the linear association, not causation or the signs of individual values.

Question 4

Skills: Select mathematical facts and techniques · Pearson correlation and regression

A model predicts y=3x+10. What change in predicted y follows an increase of 4 in x? Choose the closest value.

Show the correct answer and explanation

Correct answer: D. 12

Multiply the gradient by the change in x. The result is 12.
