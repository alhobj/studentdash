Question 1

Skills: Select mathematical facts and techniques · Arcs and sectors in degrees

A sector has radius 3 cm and angle 60°. Find its arc length in cm. Choose the closest value.

Show the correct answer and explanation

Correct answer: B. 3.1415927

Arc length = (θ/360) × 2πr. The result is 3.1415927.

Question 3

Skills: Select mathematical facts and techniques · Arcs and sectors in degrees

A sector has radius 3 cm and angle 180°. Find its perimeter in cm. Choose the closest value.

Show the correct answer and explanation

Correct answer: D. 15.424778

Add the arc length and the two radii; arc length alone is not the perimeter. The result is 15.424778.

Question 4

Skills: Select mathematical facts and techniques · Arcs and sectors in degrees

An arc is 3π cm long on a circle of radius 6 cm. Find its angle in degrees. Choose the closest value.

Show the correct answer and explanation

Correct answer: D. 90

The arc is one quarter of the circumference, so its angle is 90°. The result is 90.
