Geometric patterns

uₙ = u₁rⁿ⁻¹; Sₙ = u₁(1 − rⁿ)/(1 − r)

Here r multiplies each term to give the next. If r = 1, the total is simply n times the first term.

Question 3

Skills: Select mathematical facts and techniques · Geometric sequences and finite sums

A geometric sequence has consecutive terms 9 and 27. Find its common ratio. Choose the closest value.

Show the correct answer and explanation

Correct answer: C. 3
