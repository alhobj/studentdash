Question 1

A test gives p=0.01 at significance level 0.10. What is the decision?

Show the correct answer and explanation

Correct answer: D. Reject the null hypothesis

Each stated p-value is below 0.10. Reject H₀ at this significance level; this does not prove the alternative.

Question 2

An independence table has row total 10, column total 20 and grand total 100. Find the expected count in that cell. Choose the closest value.

Show the correct answer and explanation

Correct answer: C. 2

Under independence, expected count = row total × column total / grand total. The result is 2.

Question 3

Find the degrees of freedom for a chi-square independence test with 2 rows and 3 columns. Choose the closest value.

Show the correct answer and explanation

Correct answer: A. 2

Use (number of rows−1)(number of columns−1). The result is 2.

Question 4

Two independent groups, with sample sizes 21 and 26, are compared using a two-sided t-test of population means. Which null hypothesis is appropriate?

Show the correct answer and explanation

Correct answer: A. The two population means are equal

The null concerns the population means, not equality of observed sample means.
