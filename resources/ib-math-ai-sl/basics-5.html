Average rate of change

Average rate = change in output ÷ change in input. A derivative is a limiting rate.

Antiderivatives and area

∫xⁿ dx = xⁿ⁺¹/(n + 1) + C, n ≠ −1

Increase the power and divide by the new power; this rule excludes n = −1. Use a boundary condition to find C, or evaluate between limits for a definite integral.
