Question 1

Skills: Select mathematical facts and techniques · Perpendicular bisectors

Find the x-coordinate of the midpoint of (1,2) and (7,8). Choose the closest value.

Show the correct answer and explanation

Correct answer: A. 4

Average the endpoint x-coordinates. The result is 4.

Question 2

Skills: Select mathematical facts and techniques · Perpendicular bisectors

A segment has gradient 3. Find the gradient of its perpendicular bisector. Choose the closest value.

Show the correct answer and explanation

Correct answer: B. -0.33333333

The perpendicular gradient is the negative reciprocal. The result is -0.33333333.

Question 3

Skills: Select mathematical facts and techniques · Perpendicular bisectors

The perpendicular bisector of the segment from (1,0) to (9,0) has equation x=k. Find k. Choose the closest value.

Show the correct answer and explanation

Correct answer: D. 5

A horizontal segment has a vertical perpendicular bisector through its midpoint. The result is 5.

Question 4
